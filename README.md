# Awesome-Computer-Aided-Dispatch

# Top Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Public Safety Dispatch, 911 Call Handling, Unit Tracking, AVL, Multi-Agency Coordination & Emergency Response*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD)**. These systems help 911 centers and public safety agencies manage calls for service, recommend and dispatch units, track resources in real time, and coordinate multi-agency responses.

**Examples** include CentralSquare CAD, Hexagon CAD, Motorola PremierOne, Cyrun CAD, Tyler CAD, Mark43 CAD, RapidDeploy, Carbyne, Priority Dispatch, and GeoConex (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise public-safety CAD is almost entirely commercial due to reliability, certification, and integration requirements. Meaningful open options exist for volunteer and smaller agencies—especially **Tickets CAD** and **Resgrid**. This section expands those projects while remaining realistic about the commercial gap for large PSAPs.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[CentralSquare CAD](https://www.centralsquare.com/)**  
  Widely deployed public-safety CAD with advanced location, unit recommendation, GIS mapping, and multi-agency interoperability.

- **[Hexagon CAD](https://www.hexagon.com/)**  
  Enterprise computer-aided dispatch and public safety solutions for large agencies and integrated command centers.

- **[Motorola PremierOne CAD](https://www.motorolasolutions.com/)**  
  Dispatch-focused CAD with mapping, resource allocation, mobile incident management, and deep Motorola ecosystem integration.

- **[Cyrun CAD](https://www.cyrun.com/)**  
  Public safety CAD and related systems used by law enforcement and emergency response agencies.

- **[Tyler CAD](https://www.tylertech.com/)**  
  Computer-aided dispatch offerings within Tyler Technologies’ public safety and justice portfolio.

- **[Mark43 CAD](https://mark43.com/)**  
  Modern cloud public safety platform including CAD, records, evidence, and mobile workflows for mid-to-large agencies.

- **[RapidDeploy](https://www.rapiddeploy.com/)**  
  Cloud-native 911 and CAD-oriented platform focused on mapping, data, and next-generation emergency response.

- **[Carbyne](https://carbyne911.com/)**  
  Next-generation emergency communications and CAD-related capabilities for modern PSAPs.

- **[Priority Dispatch](https://prioritydispatch.net/)**  
  Protocol-driven emergency medical, fire, and police dispatch systems (MPDS and related) used worldwide.

- **[GeoConex](https://www.geoconex.com/)**  
  Public safety and GIS-oriented solutions supporting dispatch and location-based response workflows.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Tickets CAD](https://openises.sourceforge.net/)**  
  Free, full-featured open-source Computer-Aided Dispatch system—incident management, mapping, unit tracking, messaging, and ICS forms; long used by volunteer fire and similar organizations.

- **[Resgrid](https://github.com/Resgrid/Core)**  
  Open-source CAD, personnel, shift management, AVL, and emergency management platform (powers Resgrid.com); supports fire, EMS, and multi-agency dispatch.

- **[dispatch-dream and experimental CAD projects](https://github.com/)**  
  Modern open prototypes for multi-agency dispatch (Fire/EMS/Police) with real-time updates, geospatial routing, and APCO-oriented reference data.

- **[OpenStreetMap / Leaflet dispatch mapping stacks](https://github.com/)**  
  Open mapping foundations commonly used in open CAD systems for incident and unit visualization.

- **[APCO and public-safety code open datasets](https://github.com/)**  
  Community resources for standard incident and disposition codes used in open dispatch systems.

- **[AVL and GPS open tracking integrations](https://github.com/)**  
  Patterns for ingesting unit location from mobile devices into open CAD maps.

- **[Messaging and alerting open bridges](https://github.com/)**  
  SMS, email, chat, and radio-bridge integrations used alongside open CAD for responder notification.

- **[ICS forms and incident documentation open tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Digital ICS form templates and audit-friendly logging for volunteer and small-agency use.

- **[Documentation and Resgrid / Tickets CAD playbooks](https://docs.resgrid.com/)**  
  Guides for deploying self-hosted CAD for fire, EMS, and emergency management teams.

- **[Self-hosted PSAP-lite stacks](https://github.com/)**  
  Combined open CAD + mapping + messaging patterns for organizations that cannot afford commercial systems.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Deploying **Tickets CAD** or **Resgrid** for volunteer fire, EMS, SAR, and small agencies.
- Extending open CAD with real-time maps (Leaflet/OSM) and basic AVL.
- Accepting that NG911 compliance, deep radio/console integration, multi-jurisdiction reliability SLAs, certified protocols, and large PSAP scale still require commercial CAD (CentralSquare, Motorola PremierOne, Hexagon, Mark43, Tyler, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on cost-free capability for underserved agencies and transparent, auditable dispatch logs.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run Tickets CAD or Resgrid → integrate open maps and AVL → add messaging/alerting → document with ICS forms → keep full audit trails. Suitable for volunteer departments and smaller agencies with technical support. Primary PSAPs and large metro agencies almost always run commercial CAD.
